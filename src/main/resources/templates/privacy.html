Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

ASMJ Biz Connect collects personal and business information when you register, communicate, post commercial offers, or upload files:

  • Account Data: Username, display name, email address, encrypted password, and optional profile bio/avatars.
  • Communications & Attachments: Direct messages, group chat records, and uploaded files/images.
  • Marketplace & Advertisements: Campaign creatives, budget data, landing URLs, and inquiry interactions.
  • Resume Data: Spreadsheet and photo uploads processed in-memory for PDF generation.

2. How Information is Used

  • To provide live STOMP WebSocket messaging and instant notification delivery.
  • To enforce compliance and review commercial marketplace advertisements prior to public publishing.
  • To generate printable PDF resumes according to user-selected templates.
  • To secure platform integrity, authenticate sessions, and prevent spam or malicious interactions.

3. Data Retention & Security

All sensitive information, including user credentials, is encrypted using industry-standard BCrypt hashing algorithms. Uploaded files and media are secured in designated storage volumes, and data is not sold to third-party brokers.

4. Contact Us

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact the compliance administration team at privacy@asmjbizconnect.com.
